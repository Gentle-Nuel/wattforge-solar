How It Works

Six steps from first message to ongoing support.

1

Enquiry

Message us on WhatsApp with your location and what you need power for. Use the load calculator on the Contact page if you're not sure where to start.

2

Site Assessment

We review your site and existing load to confirm what system fits. Roof type, available space and current appliance usage all factor in.

3

Quotation

You receive a quote based on your specific site and components, not a generic price list.

4

Installation

Our team installs the system on site, including mounting adapted to your actual roof type.

5

Commissioning & Handover

The system is tested and switched on with you present, and handed over ready to use.

6

Ongoing Support

We remain reachable on WhatsApp after handover for questions or follow-up work.
