The process

How it works.

Six steps, start to finish. No step here is skipped to save time, and none of them require you to chase us for an update.

01–06

From enquiry to handover

01

Enquiry

Same day, usually within hours

You message us on WhatsApp with your location and what you're trying to power. We respond directly: no call center, no ticket number.

02

Site assessment

Within 48 hours of enquiry

We visit, or review photos and details you send for smaller jobs, to confirm roof type, wiring condition, and your actual load before quoting anything.

03

Quotation

Within 24 hours of assessment

A written quote with named components (inverter, battery, panel brand and count) and a firm price, not a range that grows once work starts.

04

Installation

2–7 days, by system size

Panels, inverter, batteries, and wiring go in. Home systems typically take 2 to 3 days; commercial systems run 5 to 7 days.

05

Commissioning & handover

Same day install completes

We test the full system under real load, walk you through what does what, and hand over documentation the same day installation finishes.

06

Ongoing support

For the life of the system

Warranty claims, troubleshooting, and future system expansions go through the same WhatsApp line you already have, not a new support portal.

The fine print

Warranty, payment, and track record.

Warranty

  • Jinko panels: 10-year product warranty
  • Deye / SRNE inverters: 5-year manufacturer warranty
  • Deriy lithium batteries: 3-year manufacturer warranty
  • Wattforge workmanship: 1-year installation warranty

Payment

  • 50% deposit to begin procurement
  • 50% balance due on commissioning
  • Bank transfer or POS, on-site
  • Commercial jobs: staged payment against milestones, arranged directly

Track record

  • Established 2018
  • 200+ systems installed nationwide
  • 3.5kVA to 30kVA, home to commercial
  • Five jobs shown in full on Our Work
