What we install.

Six services, one crew. Every module below is a service we run ourselves, end to end, never subcontracted out.

Service modules

Solar power systems

Off-grid and hybrid solar power systems, sized to your actual measured load rather than a generic package size. Off-grid suits sites with no reliable grid connection to fall back on; hybrid suits sites that want solar and battery working alongside grid power, switching automatically.

Used inEnuguElekoLekkiAwka

Inverters & battery storage

Deye and SRNE inverters paired with Deriy lithium battery banks. Deye covers most of our residential hybrid setups; SRNE handles our larger commercial installs, where higher surge loads and longer duty cycles matter more.

BrandsDeyeSRNEDeriy lithium

Solar panels

Jinko panels across nearly every install we run, chosen for consistent output and predictable lead times over chasing whichever panel is cheapest that month.

Electrical installations

Full electrical fit-outs and wiring, from a single consumer unit swap to a full commercial rewire. Our zero-damage slate-roof mount in Oregun is the reference job we point to for sites everyone else calls too difficult to touch.

Used inOregun: slate-roof mount

CCTV & security camera systems

Camera systems sized to the property, from single-entry coverage to full perimeter monitoring. Usually bundled into a solar or electrical visit so you're not paying for a second site call.

Satellite TV installation

Satellite TV setup and dish alignment, usually run alongside your electrical or solar job rather than booked as a separate visit.
