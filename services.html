What we install

Services

Six services, one crew. Tap any row for the full detail.

We design and install both off-grid and hybrid solar power systems, sized to the load you actually run rather than a generic package. Off-grid setups carry a property entirely on solar and battery; hybrid setups blend solar, battery and grid or generator power so you're covered whichever source is available. Every system is specified around real appliance counts, from single-family homes to multi-room hotels and commercial buildings.
Off-gridHybrid
We install Deye and SRNE inverters, matched to Deriy lithium battery banks, sized from single-digit kVA homes up to 30kVA commercial installations. Lithium storage means faster charge cycles and a longer working life than older battery chemistries, and hybrid inverter switching keeps supply stable across solar, battery and grid or generator input without manual intervention.

Jinko panels are our most frequently used module across residential and commercial roofs, chosen for consistent availability and output. Array sizing follows the same rule as the rest of the system: matched to real load and roof space, not oversold. Mounting is adapted to the actual roof type on site, including slate and other awkward surfaces, so the array goes up without damaging the roof underneath.
Jinko
Full electrical fit-outs and wiring for residential, commercial and retail sites: distribution, circuits and fixtures done to a standard that also has to support the solar and inverter systems we install. Our Oregun retail fit-out is a good reference point: a zero-damage slate-roof mount on a notoriously difficult roof type, executed cleanly.
Fit-outsWiring
Camera systems for homes, retail and commercial premises, installed alongside or independent of a power system. Coverage is planned around entry points and blind spots on the actual property rather than a fixed camera count, and cabling is run to match the same neat standard as our electrical work.
