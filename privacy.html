Privacy Policy.

Short version: this site doesn't run analytics, doesn't use cookies, and has nothing to sell you through a form. Here's the full version anyway.

Last updated: July 2026

What this site is

This is the website for Twinstech Solar (registered as TWINSTECK INTEGRATED SERVICES), a solar, inverter, electrical, CCTV and satellite-TV installer based in Lagos, Nigeria, operating nationwide. This policy covers this website only, not the separate agreement you'd sign for an actual installation job.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. This site has no sign-up forms, no accounts, no comment sections, and no analytics or tracking scripts of any kind, no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, nothing. We don't know you visited unless you message us.

The load calculator

The appliance calculator on the Contact page runs entirely in your browser. Your appliance counts are never sent to us, or to anyone, unless you tap "Send this to WhatsApp" yourself. Doing that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; from that point, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to the conversation, the same as any WhatsApp chat.

Local storage, not cookies

This site sets no cookies. It stores exactly one thing in your browser's local storage: whether you last viewed the site in light or dark mode, so it doesn't flip back on your next visit. That value stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Fonts and assets

All fonts, images and scripts on this site are hosted directly by us. We don't load fonts from Google Fonts or similar services, which is a common way visitor IP addresses get shared with third parties without anyone noticing. Nothing here is fetched from a third-party CDN.

Third-party links

This site links out to WhatsApp, email, and X (Twitter). Once you follow one of those links, you've left this site, and the privacy policy of whichever platform you're now on applies, not this one.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them, in practice, we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, per above.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time to reset the light/dark preference.
  • Use your browser's own tracking protections if you'd like, though this site has nothing for them to block.

Applicable law

This site is operated from Lagos, Nigeria. Where relevant, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) applies to how personal data is handled. As set out above, this site is built to collect as little personal data as possible in the first place.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
